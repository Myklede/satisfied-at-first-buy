## Cloth solver fix (agent S, 2026-11-12 ~02:30–04:00) — not live, gates above
**Root cause (measured, `.cache/solver/*.mjs`).** With rest == initial the mean drop over the first
0.17 s matched free fall (−10.7 cm vs ½gt² = −13.6 cm) and edges stretched 12× by frame 10. No
penetration: collision was not the cause. One Gauss–Seidel sweep over 2 substeps makes a hanging
chain of N rows sag about N²·g·h²/2 (≈ 50 cm for 40 rows at h = 1/120 s). Measured mean drop at
1 s: 2 substeps −52.5 cm, 8 → −5.0, 20 → −1.7, 40 → −0.8. The two-way tethers made it worse
because each root (~200 tethers) was dragged off the shoulders. Two further faults: (1) the
dihedral bend constraint gained energy from 0.1 mm noise at every compliance tested (gradient is
correct to 0.1 % vs central differences, so the cause is Gauss–Seidel overshoot on a stiff
non-linear angle); (2) the old hood tip row had 0.9 mm edges that any motion stretched 2×.

**Fix (`ClothSolver.ts` small-steps path, `SHELL_CLOTH_CONFIG`):**
- 10 substeps × (1 forward + 1 reverse stretch sweep), lambdas reset every substep.
- One-way LRA tethers: hard bound = rest geodesic × stretchMax, soft one-sided pull towards the
  built root distance. The roots are held at body anchors (static shoulder friction).
- Hinge (cross-edge) bending replaces the dihedral angle. Its rest is the wrapped shape.
- Velocity-level Coulomb friction relative to the skin; the projection push never launches.
- Cloth-on-cloth layer pairs: vertex and edge midpoint against a triangle, with remembered side,
  for sleeve↔torso and hood↔back. A stale-pair trust region stops snaps.
- Hood keeps the back shell's clearance. The cuff band has a hand stop. Elastic bands get their own
  uncalibrated limit (1.10). A short hot-list strain limit runs before contact.
- Hood tip rows widened in `shell.py`; the static gates still pass.

**Collider interface for P2 (`cloth/collider.ts`).** The solver calls `BodyCollider.distance(x, y, z,

(skinned shoulder anchors, `rootIds` order) and `rebuildLayerPairs()` after large motion.

### 1. Raised arms overstretched the jacket

**Root cause (measured).**
- **Which solver steps add the stretch.** Measured on reach_up_both S, frames 6–14, as extra
  length added per frame on edges above 1.05:
  - hinge bending: +0.5 to 3.6 m;
  - body contact: +0.07 to 1.5 m;
  - rings: up to +0.9 m.

  The stretch constraint and the hot-edge limit removed it again but never caught up. The hinges
  (1e-3 m/N) are as stiff as the stretch constraint, so the wrapped shape could not change when
  the cap and armhole needed to.
- **It is not a lag.** With the body frozen at frame 24 for 2 s, edges above 1.10 stayed at
  2,100 → 2,130. Turning parts off during that hold gave:

  | turned off | edges above 1.10 |
  | --- | --- |
  | body contact | 232 |
  | hinges | 799 |
  | layer pairs, tethers, rings or roots | about unchanged |

  With hinges off, 40 and 80 substeps still left 1,376–1,520 edges above 1.10 at frame 40.
- **Geometry: size S is smaller than the posed body.**
  - The S bicep has −0.2 cm inner ease; the fit calls it "tighter than the arm". The S bicep ring
    went from 33.7 to 40.6 cm, with points 1.6 cm inside the arm.
  - The chest ring went from 102.8 to 108.5 cm (M: 110.3 → 123.6 cm), 0.5 cm from the skin all
    round. Higher up, the rising jacket meets a wider torso (lats and armpit).

  The hard contact projection ran last in every substep, so contact always beat the fabric
  limit.
- **Candidates ruled out:**
  - Push-out by fast-moving skin: P2 found 4× slower playback made no difference, and the frozen
    body keeps the stretch.
  - Pinching in the armpit: of 1,154 stretched particles, only about 90 had two body parts within
    2 cm.
  - Missing velocity-level collision: friction already works relative to the skin, and friction
    0 was worse.
  - Root anchors: turning them off did not change the strain, but they kept the hem from rising
    (see below).
- **Shell slivers, for agent G (`shell.py`).** The sleeve caps have sub-2 mm edges: 12 on panel
  4 and 4 on panel 5.
  - Examples: 0.26 mm (5942–5943, at −0.281, 1.234, 0.070), 0.50 mm (5962–5963) and 0.78 mm
    (4411–4412).
  - They cause the 600–1,100 % "peak strain" values.
  - Of the 92 most stretched edges at reach_up_both frame 90, 58 were shorter than 6 mm, on the
    cap at the top of the shoulder.

**Fix.** The changes are in `ClothSolver.ts` and `MOTION_FIT.solver` (`engine/motion/config.ts`)
and apply to motion bakes only. The static relax and the hand pull keep their settings; an A/B
check gave bit-identical results.
- **Compliant contact above a 4 mm hard floor** (`contactCompliance` 0.3 m/N, `contactHardMin`
  −0.004 m). Where the jacket is too small, the lining sinks a few mm into tissue and fill instead
  of stretching.
- **`finalStrainPasses` 16:** a hard strain limit runs after contact, so the fabric limit wins.
  Its pull into the body is added to the contact depth, so friction grips with that load

- **Soft hinges** (1 m/N) in motion.
- **Hard contact for elastic cuffs and hem** (`contactHardElastic`). With compliant cuffs, the
  forearm slid out of the sleeve in cross_arms.
- **`rootLiftFree`:** the shoulder anchors hold the yoke from below only, so raised arms can lift

- **The hand stop follows the arm.**

## How it works
- **Mapping / cursor (A)** `engine/hand/mapping.ts`, `HandPullController.ts`: centred 70 % box of
  the (mirrored) camera frame → whole viewport; ring cursor drawn every render frame (30 ms
  exponential follow, no jumps): white open, ring filling with `grabProgress` (amber when
  `gripConfidence` is ambiguous), green grabbing, amber + dashed band to the patch while the
  fabric holds the hand back, red flash when the hand closed off the jacket.
- **Grab gate (B)**: a pinch `start` grabs only if a ray at the cursor hits the garment mesh, or a
  visible garment vertex lies within 24 px (confirmed by a ray landing ≤ 1.5 cm from it). Otherwise
  the pinch is ignored to its end. One grab at a time (tracker default; other hands ignored).
- **Patch (B)**: hit triangle + vertices reachable through the mesh within 2.5 cm (so a sleeve or
  the back layer close in space is not grabbed), weight 1 → 0.35 (quadratic), no single-vertex spike.
- **Displacement (B)**: screen plane 1:1 at the grab depth + `depthDelta` × 0.5 along the view
  direction, clamped ±6 cm; total ≤ 15 cm; target speed ≤ 1.2 m/s.
- **Strain clamp (B)** `engine/hand/pullDriver.ts`: covers the patch-border edges (midpoint
  within 8 cm of the grab). Each 60 Hz frame steps the region with the candidate target; if any
  covered edge is longer than max(rest × (its limit + 0.02), its length at grab start) — limit =
  the fabric's `stretchMax` (1.03) or `elasticStretchMax` on rib — the step is undone and retried
  with the last accepted pull (then ×0.75). The grab slips; the reach probe halves and pauses at
  the limit. Pre-grab residue there is cleaned first (60 passes); what cannot be (pinned against
  the kinematic remainder) may stay but never grows. Region-wide strain is reported, not clamped.
- **Local high-rate region (C)** `ClothSolver.setRegion/clearRegion` (new, backward compatible;
  `setCollider`, `setRootAnchors`, `rebuildLayerPairs`, `step`, `snapshot` unchanged): particles
  within **18 cm Euclidean** of the grab run the full small-steps solver (10 substeps, 600 Hz) at
  60 Hz; the outer 5 cm band is attached to its start state with a pull ramping 0 → 1 (no seam);
  the rest is kinematic, and so is the hood (panel 7) inside the radius. Rings, cuff stops and
  particle self-contact are not run in the region; layer pairs, tethers, hinges, strain-limit
  passes and body contact run. After release the region keeps
  stepping until its kinetic energy < 1e-7 J (≥ 0.6 s, ≤ 4 s), then clears.
- **Worker loop (C)** `cloth.worker.ts`: fixed-rate 60 Hz loop owned by the worker; the main
  thread only posts the latest hand target and applies frames as they arrive (never waits).
- **Tension map (C)**: per-vertex edge strain every frame, blue → red (red = at the clamp), weighted
  by distance to the grab (0 at 18 cm), full while pulling, fading over 1.5 s after release;
  grabbed patch tinted green.
